Privacy policy

Life on the Mac is used by one person, its developer, with his own Google account. No one else uses it.

What it reads

With the permissions granted at sign-in it reads Gmail messages (read only), contacts (read only) and the calendar list, and it reads and writes calendar events in the owner's own calendars.

Where the data goes

The data stays on the owner's own computer at home and in his own encrypted backup. It is not sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and it is not used for advertising or to train AI models. The use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, and the stored data is then deleted from the computer.
